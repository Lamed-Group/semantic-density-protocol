# Experimental extensions

These dimensions are recorded separately from P_observed in v0.1.

## Longevity: T

Describes demonstrated or projected survival of a construct over time. Historical survival and projected survival must be distinguished.

## Instrumentality

Measures whether the construct changes possible action, not merely interpretation.

## Strategic operability

Records whether a construct provides a reusable decision or navigation scheme.

## Archetypal closure

Describes whether the construct forms a compact self-reinforcing symbolic whole.

## Metaphor strength

Records compression, recall and transfer created by metaphor.

## Autobiographical authenticity

Records the role of embodied or first-person experience. It is not a truth guarantee.

## Process authenticity

Distinguishes a retrospective explanation from a trace captured during construction.

## "GY" marker

The Lamed Group "гы" marker is retained as a culturally specific release signal. It may indicate compression followed by semantic discharge, but no universal numeric increment is assigned in v0.1.

## Later-return resonance

Future datasets may record whether an observer returns to a construct after hours, days or months. This should be stored as longitudinal data rather than guessed at annotation time.
